# BLVCK MARKET

Marketing, brand visibility, and creative promotion website for BLVCK MARKET.

## Brand architecture
- Business and site name: **BLVCK MARKET** (V replaces A).
- Program inside the business: **THE THREE-PHASE PROGRAM**.
- Package options: **VISIBILITY UPGRADE**, **COMPLETE TAKEOVER**, **AUTOPILOT**. No prices are published.
- **THE BLVCK EFFECT** names the client transformation/results only — not a business or package.
- **VONTEVISUALZ** is the creative partner / production hub.
- FastCutEdits and ONYX BLVCK are separate brands and are not services of BLVCK MARKET.

## Content rules
- Approved and fixed wording lives in `src/content/site.ts`.
- No invented prices, discounts, metrics, clients, testimonials, logos, contact details, or social links.

## Inquiries
The contact form saves inquiries privately to the project database. Email notifications are not configured.
