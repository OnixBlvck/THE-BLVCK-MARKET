# Black Factory Marketing

Build a polished, responsive, premium marketing-agency website for a distinct business called BLVCK MARKET, affiliated with VonteVisualz but presented as its own brand and business. This is a NEW standalone project; do not modify the existing VonteVisualz project.

BRAND / CREATIVE DIRECTION
- Name: BLVCK MARKET. Affiliation lockup: BLVCK MARKET × VONTEVISUALZ.
- Grand-opening campaign/program wording already established: “BLVCK MARKET GRAND OPENING INTRODUCING: THE BLACK FACTORY PHASE PROGRAM”.
- The Black Factory Phase is the campaign/program identity. Use this phrase prominently as a featured program.
- Visual identity: cinematic dark luxury, near-black / black-on-black surfaces, charcoal, electric/deep blue illumination, restrained metallic silver and very restrained gold. Signature visual: glowing electric-blue rose with thorn detailing and falling blue petals. Dripping typography is a controlled accent for campaign headings only, not body copy. High-end editorial layout, strong typography, generous spacing, sharp contrast, subtle motion, premium imagery. Avoid generic corporate templates, cartoon art, clutter, cheap gradients, and horror aesthetics.
- Campaign colorways can rotate over time while preserving the identity: black/electric blue/restrained gold as primary; purple/black/gold and neon/lime green/black as alternate campaign palettes. Keep the homepage default blue/black.
- Do not invent or present unapproved slogans as established brand quotes. Use strong original copy as draft homepage copy, but preserve the approved program name exactly.
- Mobile-first and fully responsive. Excellent iPhone layout.

BUSINESS PURPOSE
BLVCK MARKET is a marketing, brand visibility, creative promotion, and business-growth operation. It helps businesses improve how they present themselves, reach potential customers, plan campaigns, identify market opportunities, and understand performance. It should sound confident, capable, direct, and commercially sharp—not exaggerated or scammy.

CORE OFFERINGS — USE THESE EXACT PACKAGE NAMES
1. VISIBILITY UPGRADE — brand/presence review, marketing presentation improvements, visibility recommendations, and a focused promotional direction.
2. COMPLETE TAKEOVER — a broader campaign engagement combining creative direction, promotional strategy, campaign development, outreach planning, and performance reporting.
3. VISIBILITY AUTOPILOT — ongoing promotional support, prospect research, outreach workflow planning, lead tracking, and recurring performance monitoring.
Present all three clearly and equally. Do not assign prices: no approved prices exist. Do not invent package deliverables beyond the descriptions above. Use “Request a proposal” CTAs.

SERVICE CAPABILITIES
- Brand presentation and creative direction
- Promotional campaign planning and execution support
- Audience and market opportunity research
- Competitor and positioning analysis
- Prospect research and qualification
- Targeted business outreach planning and workflows
- Lead and inquiry tracking
- Campaign performance analysis and reporting
- Marketing recommendations based on evidence

FOUNDING OFFER
First 10 participating businesses receive 20% off their first qualifying BLVCK MARKET package. Clearly state “First 10 participating businesses” and “20% off the first qualifying package.” Do not imply this discount is unlimited or recurring. CTA: “Claim founding-client consideration” or “Ask about the founding offer.”

MAKE YOUR BRAND IMPOSSIBLE TO IGNORE.” Label it as homepage marketing copy, not an established quotation. COPY RULES

- Never invent analytics, performance statistics, client names, reviews, testimonials, certifications, partner logos, prices, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, booking links, social handles, or integrations.
- Do not display the illustrative example prices $150/$400/$600; those were explicitly not approved.
- Do not confuse BLVCK MARKET with InkNior, FastCutEdits, or ONYX BLVCK. They are separate businesses/brands.
- Use the spelling BLVCK MARKET exactly, and VONTEVISUALZ exactly.

BUILD QUALITY
- Create the actual homepage as a functioning responsive web app, not a static image or a written proposal.

- Ensure all navigation and CTAs work: scroll to the correct sections or open the inquiry area. Form validation must work and feedback must be honest.
- Use quality visual assets fitting the approved blue rose / black luxury identity. Avoid generic stock-office photos.
- Build the complete homepage experience and supporting sections now. Do not publish/deploy or connect third-party services without authorization.
